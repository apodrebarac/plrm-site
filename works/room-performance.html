Work 03

Room Performance

Year April 2018
Medium Endurance performance
Subtitle Containment, Rupture, and the Threshold of Pain
Documentation Author and Clara Maria Lorusso

What cannot be held inside will force its way through the body.

The Room Performance unfolded across two interconnected spaces that together articulated a movement from containment to rupture. In the first room, I remained enclosed, silent, and internally recursive, holding a tension that could not yet find form. This claustrophobic state accumulated pressure without release — a contained internal environment that mirrored the body's own capacity to hold what it cannot yet speak.

In the second space, I performed actions — including burning my own text — that allowed the accumulated internal pressure to find physical release. The threshold between the two spaces marked the moment when interiority became externality, when the body could no longer contain what it held and was forced to produce it outward as action.

The burning of text was not destruction but transformation: language that could not be processed internally was given over to fire, released from its fixed form. What remained was not absence but residue — the trace of what had been held and could no longer be.

Methodological significance

This transition revealed a foundational methodological principle within PLRM: what cannot be processed internally will eventually force its way through the body. Pain became a vocabulary for the unspeakable — a means through which contradiction and emotional residue found expression. The two-room structure established a spatial logic of threshold that would recur across subsequent works: the move from containment to rupture, from internal accumulation to external release.
