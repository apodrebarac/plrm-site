Work 11 — Performance 12

Childhood Drawings, LoRA Models, and the Algorithmic Return to Repressed Memory

Period 1993–1997 (drawings) / 2024–2025 (computational)
Origin Karlovac, Croatia (war period)
Medium LoRA models, ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion, 0wl dataset
Status Ongoing

Childhood drawings made during wartime in Karlovac, 1993–1997 — used to train LoRA models that reconfigure repressed visual memory through computational synthesis.

Performance 12 is held at the level of process, not presented as finished work. It begins with two distinct datasets carrying fundamentally different visual registers: childhood drawings from Karlovac made during the war years of 1993–1997, and personal photographs spanning a lifetime. The drawings encode a child's perceptual processing of violence — bright colours, cheerful compositional logic sitting alongside bombs and planes, the impossible happiness of a child's mark-making during siege. The photographs carry the documentary register of an actual life.

Each dataset trains a separate LoRA model — low-rank matrices that adapt specific parameters of a base Stable Diffusion checkpoint, learning the visual grammar of their source material as a modular patch. The drawings LoRA learns the colour palette, line quality, and compositional logic of a wartime childhood. The photographs LoRA learns the documentary register.

Datasets

Childhood Drawings

Crayon and marker drawings made in Karlovac, Croatia, 1993–1997. Scanned at high resolution. The paper texture, colour palette, and gestural quality are what the LoRA learns — the child's perceptual encoding of wartime as visual grammar.

Personal Photographs

A curated selection of photographs spanning the lifetime of the artist. The photographic LoRA carries the documentary register — realism against which the drawings' impossible cheerfulness can collide.

ComfyUI pipeline

01 Both LoRA models are chained via Load LoRA nodes onto a base Stable Diffusion / FLUX checkpoint
02 Prompts drawn from the 0wl dataset — grandmother's letters and RNN-generated poetic fragments — are fed through the CLIP text encoder
03 The language of grief and memory literally becomes the compositional instruction that steers what the visual models produce
04 KSampler denoises and VAE decodes the latent — producing images where the child's visual logic and photographic realism collide in the uncanny space between them

Why this is process, not product

The work refuses the logic of the finished image. The LoRA strength ratios are adjusted continuously — pushing toward the drawings' colour palette or pulling toward photographic realism, or finding the uncanny space between. A lower CFG scale gives the model room to drift and produce unexpected combinations; the ambiguity in the 0wl language gives the model interpretive space. The algorithm reconfigures impossible childhood scenes into new visual forms, exposing how perception was shaped by contradiction, how memory is not stored but generated.
