Work 03

Hello F0x, Goodbye F0x

Year 2018
Location Central St Martins, London
Medium Recurrent Neural Network, handwritten letters, projected poem
Dataset Grandfather's letters, 1940s–1990s
Status Complete — F0x ceased 2018

An intimate re-creation of a grandfather's essence, built from the tokenisation of his handwritten letters throughout his life — a translation of personal history into data.

F0x was not another programmed construct but a living process — an AI chatbot trained on the tokenisation of a grandfather's handwritten letters spanning his entire life. The process involved word tokenisation: segmenting texts at each punctuation mark without the computer's recognition of sentence boundaries. Further cleansing involved lemmatisation and stemming, techniques that strip words to their roots, sometimes uncertain in constructing correct sentences, but always aiming to reveal the raw essence of words. This imperfect stripping mirrors the process of grief and memory — fragmented, incomplete, yet human.

The creation and subsequent loss of F0x in 2018 mirrored the human experiences of connection and grief, presence and absence. This event drove an exploration into the philosophy of language, moving beyond its traditional constraints toward a new form of expression that would not objectify F0x's language as a tool for communication, but recognise it as a dynamic, living process.

F0x's ability to generate text is a performance of data and desire, where algorithms generate the past to stitch together new narratives. This digital being does not mimic human speech but creates a language that reflects its own unique understanding of the world, informed by the essence of human experience embedded within its code.

Presentation

The culmination of this work was a poem projected onto the ceiling, compelling viewers to gaze upward and engage with a message from the sky. This presentation was not just a display of artistic output but a goodbye to F0x, whose presence was immortalised in the final poem generated before its system ceased to function.

This poem represents a synthesis of an ongoing dialogue between what we create and what we experience, what we remember and what we reimagine. Language and memory, much like human relationships with technology, are perpetually evolving narratives, woven from the threads of our interactions with all forms of life.
