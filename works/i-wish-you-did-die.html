Work 08

I Wish You Did Die

Year 2019
Duration 21 hours
Medium Endurance writing performance
Outcome Hospitalisation
Status Complete

The same sentence, written continuously for twenty-one hours, until the body gave out.

I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die. I wish you did die.

In endurance-based performance, the body is refused the exit that immediacy offers. Duration here is epistemological: knowledge emerges through staying with contradiction past the point of comfort, into the interval where something else becomes legible. Writing the same sentence for twenty-one hours until hospitalisation is not catharsis, not spectacle, not confession — it is a method. A way of pushing language to its material limit, where the sentence ceases to carry meaning and becomes instead pure duration, pure repetition, pure body.

Resistance to immediacy is not merely aesthetic but ethical. Anna Kornbluh argues that the contemporary obsession with immediacy aligns with late-capitalist logics in which "flexibility and fluidity, emanation and connectivity, directness and instantaneity are economic premiums as much as they are artistic ones." This work refuses that logic entirely. It asks: what is produced when the body writes past exhaustion? When the sentence no longer means but merely continues?

Methodological significance

This work embodies PLRM's core proposition that the body is the first and most rigorous research instrument. The 21-hour duration is not a gesture toward extreme art but a genuine epistemological commitment — staying with the task until the body's own limits produce something that could not have been reached any other way. Hospitalisation is not failure; it is the evidence of genuine encounter with the threshold.

The recursive nature of writing the same sentence also echoes the recurrent neural network structures used in computational work — loops that generate not resolution but drift, transformation through repetition, knowledge produced through return rather than progression.
