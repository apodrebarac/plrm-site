Work 06

Cleaning Performance

Year 2019
Medium Durational video performance
Relation Continuation of Guilt, Pain & Chained
Status Complete

A quiet act of cleaning. A durational ritual of purification and renewal.

In the aftermath of the chain performances, a week of reflective and meditative exploration followed — a closing of the intense physical pain endured. After the brutal encounter with chains burdened with the weight of honey and blood, a phase of purgative cleansing began: a visual and symbolic act of washing away the remains of pain and suffering.

This act was not a practical necessity but an essential artistic and emotional continuation. A quiet act of cleaning — a durational performance captured on video where only the hands are visible, methodically washing each link of the chain. This process transformed into a ritual of purification and renewal; each movement a careful undoing of the past. The cleansing of the chains from blood and honey became a metaphor for healing and recovery, an effort to restore purity and clarity not just to the chains but to the state of being.

The honey, initially a symbol of prosperity and the sweetness that can emerge from suffering, mixed with the blood, was a reminder of the real costs of such struggles. Cleaning was an act of reclaiming agency — an intimate and deliberate process that allowed engagement with each moment of pain and transformation, an acknowledgement and then a washing away.

Methodological significance

This series of chain performances, rooted in physicality, shows the transcending nature of one being. It invites audiences to consider and question not only the spectacle of suffering, but the quiet, often unseen acts of recovery. This work emerged from a recognition that the compelling nature of useless acts and the interhuman perspective of suffering can draw an audience into the performance — but that the aftermath, the repair, is equally the work.
